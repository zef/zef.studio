# Wooden Toy Rifle

It was a snowy day in October and my daughter wanted to dress up as
[Annie Oakley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Oakley) for Halloween,
so we decided to make her a wooden toy rifle.

I sketched out the shape on a section of 2x4, and cut it out on my bandsaw. I
used a forstner bit to drill the hole for the trigger guard.  Then I split that
in two on the bandsaw, to get two rifles from the same piece of wood.
Then finished them with a bit of sanding and oil.
