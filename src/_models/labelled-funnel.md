The interesting part of a funnel is the throat. A cone that meets a tube at a
hard corner will pack solid with anything powdery — flour, protein powder,
ground coffee — because the material arrives at a wall it cannot flow around.
Curve the transition and it keeps moving.

That curve is a bezier through three points, and the *throat curve* parameter
controls how deep the bell is. At zero you get a straight cone. Turn it up and
the wall bows out into something closer to a funnel you would buy.

The rim carries text wrapped around its circumference. Print one per material
and label them — that is the whole reason this exists as a customizer rather
than a fixed model. Bold lettering survives the print better than regular at
small rim widths.

Print it rim-down on the flat lip. No supports, and the spout comes out clean.
