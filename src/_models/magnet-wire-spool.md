I was winding pickup coils and kept guessing at how big to make the flanges,
which meant either a spool that ran out of room halfway through or one that was
comically oversized for the wire on it.

So this one does the arithmetic. Give it the wire diameter and the number of
turns you want, and it works out how many turns fit in a layer, how many layers
that needs, and how far the winding will stack up — then sizes the flanges to
clear it with a margin. The computed values under the viewer update as you
change things, including roughly how much wire to cut.

The numbers assume tidy winding. Real coils are messier than the arithmetic, so
give yourself a little headroom on the turn count.
