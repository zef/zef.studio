I have printed more of these than anything else I have ever designed. They hold
speaker wire along a baseboard, the vacuum cord inside a cabinet door, and a
whole bundle of sensor wiring under my workbench.

The parameter worth understanding is the wall thickness, which is set as a
*number of perimeters* rather than a distance. Tell it your extrusion width and
how many walls you want, and the clip comes out at a thickness your slicer can
print as solid loops — no thin-wall gap fill, no mystery voids. Two perimeters
gives a clip with some spring to it; four gives one that does not move.

The gap ratio controls the snap. Below about 0.85 the cable has to be pushed
past a pinch point and stays put on its own.
