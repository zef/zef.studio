I ran network cable between two rooms and did not want a ragged hole in the
drywall on either side. This lines the hole, covers the torn paper edge with a
flange, and grips the cable so it cannot saw back and forth against the gypsum
every time something tugs on it.

The grip is a set of short fingers pointing inward — rings with most of their
circumference cut away, so each finger can flex. Set the rib count to zero and
you get a plain smooth-bore sleeve instead, which is what you want if something
rigid is passing through.

Measure your wall. Half-inch drywall is 12.7 mm, but plaster over lath can be
double that, and the barrel needs to reach all the way through.
