I tiled a bathroom floor and ran out of spacers two rows from the end, which is
how most of my prints start. The ones you buy are fine, but they only come in
the width you bought, and the wide-format tile I was setting wanted a line
somewhere between two of the sizes on the shelf.

The ball on each arm tip is the part that matters. A plain square arm wedges
itself into the mortar and fights you on the way out; the ball touches the tile
edge on a small enough patch that the spacer lifts straight out once things
have set.

Print a dozen at a time — set the copy count and the whole row comes off the
plate together. They are worth keeping in a jar.
